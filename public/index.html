KEEP THE MAGIC A SECRET

Parent corner

Private purchase planning stays here. Children keep seeing all their wishes, including purchased and reserved gifts.

Parental permission & privacy

We store nicknames, optional ages for suggestions, wishes, profile permissions, optional budgets, retailer product links and prices, and private purchase records on this local server. There are no analytics, third-party assets, advertising cookies, or payment methods. The shared catalog includes clearly labeled fictional samples and manually recorded family entries.

Each child receives a restricted profile code. You decide whether they can see prices, budgets, and external retailer links. External sites have their own privacy policies and may offer checkout; this app cannot control those sites. Use supervised browsing when appropriate. You may optionally enter an age for suggestions; no birth date is collected. Parents can override recommendations, but mandatory legal, retailer, and app-safety exclusions still apply.

This local permission record does not verify adulthood or satisfy all requirements for a public children’s service. Deletion does not erase external backups.
